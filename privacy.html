Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This website

fakeproblemspodcast.com is a static site. It has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no comments, no cookies of our own, and no analytics or advertising trackers. We do not collect, store, or sell personal information from visitors.

Some pages embed videos from YouTube. When you play an embedded video, YouTube may set cookies and collect data under Google's privacy policy. Links to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and other platforms are governed by those platforms' own policies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data (such as IP addresses) for security and operations; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Our publishing tools

We use a private, in-house application ("Fake Problems Podcast Automation") to publish our own episodes and clips. It signs in with Google only to our own YouTube channel, and uses that access solely to:

  • upload our episodes and clips, and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and playlists;
  • read our own channel's statistics (YouTube Analytics) to see how our videos perform.

It does not access anyone else's Google account or data. The sign-in token is stored only on our own computer, is never shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can review or revoke an app's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
